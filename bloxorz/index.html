How to play

Roll each 1×1×2 brick into a hole while upright. Sink every block before the timer runs out.

  • Multiple blocks — tap or swipe the brick you want to move.
  • Upright covers 1 tile; flat covers 2. Only upright-in-hole counts as sunk.
  • No falling — moves off the board or onto empty space are rejected.
  • Timer defaults to 2:00. Timeout fails and restarts the level.
  • D-pad / arrows move the selected block. Tab or 1–9 to change selection.
  • Optional Studs toggle (default off).
